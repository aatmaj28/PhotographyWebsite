About

Aatmaj Amol Salunke

Photographer & Visual Artist

Age: 23  ·  Northeastern University, Boston

Passionate about capturing the beauty in everyday moments. My journey in photography began as a way to document life's fleeting instances, and has evolved into a medium for artistic expression. Through my lens, I strive to tell stories that resonate with viewers and evoke emotions that transcend the visual medium.

Currently pursuing my Masters in Artificial Intelligence at Northeastern University in Boston and working full-time as an AI Engineer and Researcher, I balance my professional life with my creative pursuits, finding inspiration in the vibrant city landscape and diverse cultural experiences around me.
